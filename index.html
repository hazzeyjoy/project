A place to begin

Young people are not the future. They are the now.

YIDK is a youth-focused initiative that believes lasting change starts by listening. YIDP, the Kenya-based programme represented here, was established in 2016. Its work brings together sport, education, health, digital skills, and community development so young people can learn, connect, and shape their futures.

Our mission

Help young people thrive through sport, education, health, digital skills, and enterprise, while advancing wellbeing, inclusion, and community development.

Our vision

An inclusive future where young people have the support and opportunities to lead healthy, fulfilling lives and strengthen their communities.

How we work

Listen first. Build together. Keep growing.

We believe good youth work is done with young people, not simply for them. That means listening to lived experience, making room for different perspectives, and learning as we go.

1

Start with listening

Understand people, strengths, and priorities in each community.

2

Make space for young leaders

Support young people to shape ideas and take meaningful action.

3

Grow what works

Reflect together, share learning, and keep improving our work.
